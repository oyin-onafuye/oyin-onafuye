## 📊 Featured Projects

### [Amazon Consumer Behavior Analysis](https://github.com/oyin-onafuye/amazon-consumer-behavior-analysis)
**What Actually Drives Consumer Choice in 2026 — Price or Social Proof?**

In 2026, brands like PepsiCo, Walmart, and E.l.f. are cutting prices to win back consumers. This analysis of 42,675 Amazon product observations shows that is the wrong lever. Consumers are not responding to price tags — they are responding to review count and purchase volume. Price does not signal quality. The crowd does.
